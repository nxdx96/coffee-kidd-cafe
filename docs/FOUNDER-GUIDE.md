# Updating the Coffee Kidd Cafe website

Hi Natalia & Zach — this is everything you need to run the site yourselves. No code,
no developer. You edit in one app, hit **Publish**, and the website updates.

If anything here doesn't match what you see on screen, it changed — ask and we'll
update this guide.

---

## Where you edit

Your editing app ("the Studio") lives at:

> **studio.coffeekiddcafe.com** (or the link we send you)

Log in with the **coffeekiddcafe@gmail.com** Google account. You'll see a list on the
left: **Site settings**, **Home page**, **Story page**, **Contact page**, **Mailing
list page**, **Menu items**, and **Events**.

**The golden rule:** after any change, click **Publish** (bottom right). If you don't
publish, the website won't change. To undo, there's a version history on each item.

---

## The things you'll change most

### Hours and address
**Site settings → Basics.** Type your hours (one line per row) and address. There are
"note" fields for the muted grey text like "Address posted before opening" — clear
those once the real info is in. **Publish.**

### Opening day: turn the site on
Before you open, the homepage shows a "Coming soon" page. On opening day:
**Site settings → Launch → turn OFF "Pre-launch mode" → Publish.** The full homepage
(with your photo and story) goes live. That's the one big switch.

### Photos
Any photo field: **drag an image in** from your computer. Add a short **caption** and a
**description** (the description is for people using screen readers — just say what's in
the photo). **Publish.** Landscape photos suit rooms; portrait suits people.

Until you add a real photo, the site shows a striped placeholder with a note — that's
normal, it just means "photo goes here."

### Text on any page
Open **Home / Story / Contact / Mailing list page**, edit the paragraphs (each row is
one paragraph), **Publish.**

### The menu
**Menu items → "Create".** Fill in name, section (Coffee / Tea / Matcha / Baked goods /
Art), description, price. Add one item per drink/food. As soon as you publish the first
one, "Menu, soon" in the site's navigation becomes a real **Menu** link automatically.
Mark items **seasonal** if they come and go; use **sort order** to arrange them.

### Events
**Events → "Create".** Title, date, description, optional photo. Upcoming events show
first; past ones move down on their own after the date passes.

---

## The mailing list & contact form

Sign-ups and messages **don't** live in the Studio — they go to the service we connected
(we'll show you which). You'll get contact messages by email, and manage newsletter
subscribers in that tool. Nothing to do here day to day.

---

## Good habits
- **Publish** after every change (worth repeating).
- Keep captions/descriptions short and real.
- Changes appear on the live site within a minute or two.
- Made a mistake? Open the item → version history → restore. Nothing is ever truly lost.

Questions or something looks off — email us and we'll jump in.
