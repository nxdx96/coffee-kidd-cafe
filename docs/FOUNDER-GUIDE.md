# Updating the Coffee Kidd Cafe website

Hi Natalia & Zach — this is everything you need to run the site yourselves. No code,
no developer. You type in one app, click **Publish**, and the website updates. That's it.

If anything here doesn't match what you see on screen, it changed — text us and we'll
fix the guide.

---

## Logging in

Your editing app ("the Studio") lives at:

> **https://coffeekiddcafe.sanity.studio/**

Bookmark it. To get in:

1. The first time, check your email for an **invite from Sanity** and click **Accept**.
2. Go to the link above and choose **Continue with Google**.
3. Sign in with **your own personal Google account** — the one we invited. That gives each
   of you full editing access.

(The **coffeekiddcafe@gmail.com** login is the "admin" account — it's only needed for
account/billing stuff, not day-to-day editing. For everyday changes, just use your own login.)

Once you're in, the left side lists everything you can edit: **Site settings**, **Home page**,
**Story page**, **Contact page**, **Mailing list page**, **Menu items**, and **Events**.

---

## The one rule that matters

After **any** change, click the **Publish** button (bottom-right of the screen).

- No Publish = the website doesn't change. The blue "Publish" button means you have unsaved
  edits; when it goes grey and says "Published," you're live.
- Changed your mind? Every item has a **history** (the ⋯ menu, top-right) — you can look at
  or restore any earlier version. **Nothing is ever truly lost**, so don't be afraid to try things.

### Try this first (2 minutes, to get comfortable)
Open **Site settings → Basics**, change the tagline slightly, click **Publish**, wait a minute,
refresh the website. See it change, then change it back. That's the whole loop — everything else
is just variations on it.

## The things you'll change most

### Hours and address
**Site settings → Basics** (the first tab). Type your address and hours — **one line per row**
(click "Add item" for another line). The grey "note" fields (like *Address posted before opening*)
are the faded placeholder text on the site; empty them once the real info is in. **Publish.**

### Photos
Any **Photo** field: click it and **drag an image in** from your computer (or click to browse).
Add a short **Caption** and a **Description** (the description is just "what's in the photo," for
people using screen readers). **Publish.**
- Wide photos suit rooms; tall photos suit people.
- Before you add a real photo, the site shows a striped grey box with a note — that's normal, it
  just means "photo goes here."

### Text on any page
Open **Home / Story / Contact / Mailing list page** and edit the paragraphs (each row is one
paragraph). **Publish.**

### The menu
**Menu items → the pencil/＋ "Create" button.** Fill in **Name**, **Section** (pick Coffee / Tea /
Matcha / Baked goods / Art), **Description**, **Price**. One entry per drink or food. **Publish.**
- The moment you publish your **first** item, "Menu, soon" in the site's top menu turns into a real
  **Menu** link — automatically.
- Tick **Seasonal** for things that come and go. Use **Sort order** (lower number shows first) to
  arrange them.

### Events
**Events → "Create".** Add a **Title**, **Date**, **Description**, and an optional **Photo**.
**Publish.** Upcoming events show first; once an event's date passes, it moves down to "Past" on
its own.

## Opening day — the one big switch

Before you open, the homepage is the **"Coming soon"** page on purpose. When you're ready to open
the full site:

1. **Site settings → Launch** tab.
2. Turn **Pre-launch mode → OFF**.
3. **Publish.**

That swaps the homepage from "Coming soon" to the full site (photo, story, the works). Before you
flip it, it's worth having your **hours**, **address**, and a few **menu items** filled in so the
site looks complete the moment it goes live.

## Quick habits
- **Publish** after every change (worth saying twice).
- Keep captions and descriptions short and real.
- Edits show up on the live site within a minute or two — refresh the page to see them.
- Stuck, or something looks wrong? Text us. You can't break it — worst case we restore a version.
